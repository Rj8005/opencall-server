Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy applies to everything you buy from OpenCall, operated by Rahul Jha, Bhagalpur, Bihar, India. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Numbers

Number rentals are non-refundable once the number has been assigned to your account.

We rent each number from an upstream carrier at the moment you buy it, and that cost is incurred immediately and is not returned to us if you change your mind. Once a number is assigned it is yours for the rental period and cannot be returned for credit.

Before you buy, please check:

  • The number is shown to you before payment. Confirm the country and the digits.
  • Numbers cannot receive verification codes from WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, banks and most services that send one-time passwords. If that is why you want a number, do not buy one.
  • Numbers cannot call emergency services.
  • A number only rings while you are signed in on a device that is connected.

Minute packs

Minute packs are non-refundable once purchased, because minutes become immediately usable.

Minutes expire twelve months after purchase. They cannot be withdrawn as cash, transferred to another user, or exchanged for anything other than calls on the Service.

When we will refund

We will refund you in full, without argument, in these cases:

  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • You paid but received nothing â€” payment succeeded and no number was assigned or no minutes were credited.
  • We withdrew your number during a paid period because a carrier or regulator required it, and you did not cause the withdrawal. We refund the unused part of that period.
  • We discontinued the Service during a paid period. We refund the unused part.
  • A charge you did not authorise. Tell us and we will investigate and refund if confirmed.

When we will not refund

  • You changed your mind after the number was assigned.
  • You bought the wrong country or the wrong number.
  • The number was rejected by WhatsApp, Telegram, a bank or any other service. This is disclosed before purchase and is a property of all virtual numbers.
  • You lost access to your identity and had no backup. We cannot recover it and the purchase stands.
  • You did not use the number or the minutes.
  • Your account was suspended for breaching our Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Call quality was affected by your own internet connection or device.
  • Minutes expired unused after twelve months.

Cancelling

You can release a number from within the app at any time. Releasing stops future renewals. It does not refund the current period, and the number is returned to the carrier immediately and cannot be reclaimed.

Automatic renewal is off by default. If you turn it on, you can turn it off at any time before the renewal date.

How to request a refund

Write to support@opencall.space with your OpenCall address, the payment reference from Razorpay or PayPal, the date, and what went wrong.

  • We aim to reply within 3 working days.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days.
  • Razorpay and PayPal may take a further 5 to 10 working days to credit your account.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank or card issuer. Most problems are a fault we can fix quickly. If you raise a chargeback, we may suspend the account while the dispute is resolved.
