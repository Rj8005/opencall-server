Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy forms part of our Terms of Service. It exists because telecom fraud and abuse are real, because our carriers require us to enforce it, and because a single abusive account can get the Service shut down for everyone.

You must not use OpenCall to

Break the law

  • Do anything illegal under Indian law, the law where you are, or the law of the country you are calling.
  • Violate telecommunications regulations. In India this includes operating an unauthorised telephone exchange, bypassing licensed international gateways, or terminating international traffic as domestic traffic.
  • Evade lawful interception or falsify records.

Commit fraud or abuse the network

  • Toll fraud, traffic pumping, artificial inflation of traffic, or dialling high-cost destinations to generate revenue.
  • Using stolen or unauthorised payment methods.
  • Sharing or reselling access to your account.
  • Automated or bulk dialling of any kind, including robocalling and war dialling.
  • Deliberately overloading our servers, or attempting to circumvent rate limits, spending limits or destination restrictions.
  • Attempting to access another user's account, identity or data.
  • Probing, scanning or testing the security of our systems without written permission.

Misrepresent who you are

  • Presenting a caller ID you do not have the right to use.
  • Impersonating another person, a business, a bank, a government body or a public official.
  • Any form of caller ID spoofing intended to deceive.

Harm or harass people

  • Threats, stalking, intimidation, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Hate speech or incitement to violence against any person or group.
  • Anything involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children. We report this to the authorities without exception.
  • Distributing malware, phishing for credentials, or running scams of any kind.
  • Unsolicited commercial calls or messages.

Misuse the offline mesh

The offline mesh feature is provided for use where networks are unavailable or unreliable â€” hiking and remote travel, disasters, events and crowded venues. It must not be used to coordinate criminal activity or to evade lawful investigation.

What you should also know

No emergency calls. OpenCall cannot reach emergency services anywhere. Keep a working phone.

  • Some destinations are blocked and cannot be dialled. This is deliberate â€” high-cost and premium-rate ranges are the usual targets of toll fraud.
  • We apply spending and concurrency limits to every account.
  • Call metadata is retained for 12 months, as described in our Privacy Policy, and is used to detect fraud.

How we enforce this

We monitor for patterns that indicate fraud â€” unusual destinations, unusual volume, unusual hours, repeated failed calls. We do not listen to calls and we cannot read message content.

Where we find a breach we may, depending on how serious it is:

  • Warn you and ask you to stop
  • Restrict destinations or lower your limits
  • Suspend the account while we investigate
  • Terminate the account and release the number, without refund
  • Report the matter to the authorities

For suspected fraud or anything involving harm to a child, we act immediately and without warning.

Reporting abuse

If you receive an abusive call from an OpenCall number, or believe someone is misusing the Service, write to support@opencall.space with the number involved, the date and time, and what happened. We investigate every report.
