Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

OpenCall is operated by Rahul Jha, an individual based in Bhagalpur, Bihar, India. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not collect. Questions go to support@opencall.space.

The short version. Your identity keys are generated on your device and never sent to us. We cannot read your messages or listen to your calls. We keep the minimum needed to route calls, bill you, and let you recover your identity if you ask us to hold an encrypted backup.

2. What we never collect

  • Your private key or recovery phrase. These are generated on your device and never transmitted.
  • The content of your calls or messages. Calls between OpenCall users are end-to-end encrypted and travel directly between devices wherever possible. Offline mesh traffic never touches our servers at all.
  • Call audio. We do not record calls. Recording is disabled at our carriers.
  • Your card or bank details. These go directly to Razorpay or PayPal. We only receive a reference and a result.
  • Your contacts. Contacts stay on your device.
  • Your location.

3. Google Sign-In and the identity backup

If you choose to back up your identity with Google, this is what happens:

  • Google tells us three things: a permanent account identifier, your email address, and whether that email is verified. We request nothing else â€” no access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.
  • Your device encrypts your identity with a password you choose, using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from that password. The encryption happens entirely on your device.
  • We store the resulting encrypted blob against your Google account identifier.

We cannot decrypt this backup. We never receive your password and there is no recovery route through us. If you forget the password, the backup is unusable â€” use your 12-word recovery phrase instead.

You can delete the backup at any time from the backup screen in the app. This removes the encrypted blob, your email address and the account identifier from our systems permanently. We do not use your email for marketing.
